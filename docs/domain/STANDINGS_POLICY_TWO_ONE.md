# Standings policy: Two points for a win, one point for a loss

## Scope

This is the initial standings policy for the first Rally league-stage implementation. It is separate from `MatchFormat` and may be replaced by a different policy in another competition regulation.

## Rule

For every confirmed, played team match:

- the winning team receives 2 standings points;
- the losing team receives 1 standings point.

The points allocation is independent of the final team-match score. A 4:0, 4:1, 4:2, and 4:3 win all award 2 points to the winner and 1 point to the loser.

## Deferred cases

The policy does not yet define points for retirements, walkovers, forfeits, no-shows, voided matches, or disciplinary decisions. Those cases remain deferred until their result states are specified.
